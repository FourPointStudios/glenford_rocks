# Glenford Rocks Links Page Inventory

Inventory date: September 10, 2026

## Scope

This inventory was recovered from the clean November 28, 2021 Internet
Archive capture of the original Glenford Rocks Links page:

https://web.archive.org/web/20211128172408/https://glenfordrocks.com/links/

The archived page contains 17 external resource links. No links from the
currently compromised `glenfordrocks.com` site were used as source material.

## Summary

- 11 links are suitable to retain at their current destinations.
- 5 links should be updated to a more precise, secure, current, or archived
  destination before publication.
- 1 old destination is unsafe and must not be reused. It now redirects to an
  unrelated gambling site.
- 4 additional lines on the old page are headings or captions, not links.
